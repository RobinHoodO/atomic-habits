# Atomic Habits

A habit system for people who want to build good habits, alone and together.

## Language

**Home**:
A shared space where the people who live together build good habits as a team.
_Avoid_: Household, shared habit space, space

**Routine**:
Something that repeats. It belongs to one person or to the whole Home.
_Avoid_: Chore, recurring task

**Task**:
Something that is done one time.
_Avoid_: To-do, one-off, engangsoppgave

**Repeat rule**:
How a Routine comes back. Two kinds: **Etter utført** (a set time after it was last done) and **Faste dager** (on set weekdays). A Routine with **Ved behov** has no date.
_Avoid_: Cadence, frequency, recurrence

**Owner**:
Who does a Routine. Three kinds: one person, **Bytter på**, or **Felles**.
_Avoid_: Assignee, responsible

**Bytter på**:
The two people take turns owning a Routine. The next round always goes to the person who did not do the last one.
_Avoid_: Rotating, alternating

**Felles**:
A Routine with no owner. Whoever does it first has done it. It can carry a short "Den som …" note.
_Avoid_: Open, shared, conditional

**Gi bort**:
Hand one round of a Routine to the other person. After that round, the Routine goes back to its Owner.
_Avoid_: Delegate, reassign, transfer

**Hopp over**:
Skip one round of a Routine. It does not count as done.
_Avoid_: Skip, dismiss

**Utsett**:
Move a Routine to a later day. It is still the same round.
_Avoid_: Postpone, snooze

**I dag**:
The one list a person opens each day: what is due or late for them, plus Felles things. The other person's things sit in a closed part below.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, feed, agenda

**Senere**:
A Task with no day yet. A Senere Task older than 2 weeks shows up in I dag at the weekend.
_Avoid_: Backlog, someday, inbox

**Poeng**:
What a person earns for doing a Routine or a Task. Each thing has its own number. A Felles Routine gives one extra.
_Avoid_: XP, score, credits

**Premie**:
A prize the two people agree on, with a price in Poeng, e.g. "Massasje" for 10. One person pays with Poeng (**Løs inn**); the other person gives it and marks it **Gitt**.
_Avoid_: Reward, perk, shop item
